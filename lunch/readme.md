# California Rolls

## Description

California rolls filled with imitation crab, avocado, and cucumber, wrapped in nori with seasoned sushi rice on the outside and topped with toasted sesame seeds. Serve with soy sauce, wasabi, and optional spicy mayo.

### Ingredients:

- [ ] 2 cups Japanese short- or medium-grain rice
- [ ] 2 cups cold water for the stovetop method OR 2½ cups for the rice maker method
- [ ] 5 tbsp prepared sushi vinegar OR the homemade sushi vinegar mixture below
- [ ] 4 tbsp rice vinegar, for homemade sushi vinegar
- [ ] 2 tbsp sugar, for homemade sushi vinegar
- [ ] 2 tsp salt, for homemade sushi vinegar
- [ ] ½ lb imitation crab meat
- [ ] 1 avocado, ripe but firm
- [ ] ½ medium cucumber, peeled and cut into long julienne strips
- [ ] Toasted nori seaweed sheets
- [ ] Toasted sesame seeds
- [ ] Soy sauce, for serving
- [ ] Wasabi paste or prepared wasabi powder, for serving
- [ ] Approximately 1 tbsp mayonnaise, for optional spicy mayo
- [ ] Approximately 1 tsp Sriracha, or to taste, for optional spicy mayo

### Steps for preparation:

1. If making homemade sushi vinegar, combine the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt in a small saucepan. Heat gently until dissolved, then let cool. Use this mixture instead of the prepared sushi vinegar.
2. Rinse the rice with cold water until the water runs clear, then drain well.
3. Cook the rice using one of these methods:
   - Rice maker: Add the drained rice and 2½ cups cold water. Cook on the white rice setting according to the appliance instructions.
   - Stovetop: Add the drained rice and 2 cups cold water to a heavy saucepan. Cover tightly and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce to medium and cook, covered, until the water is absorbed, approximately 7–8 minutes. Without lifting the lid, listen for the bubbling to stop. Reduce the heat to very low and cook for another 6 minutes. Remove from the heat and let stand, covered, for 15 minutes.
4. Transfer the hot rice to a large bowl and gently break up any clumps. Let it cool until warm, then stir in the cooled sushi vinegar.
5. Cover a sushi rolling mat with plastic wrap. Fold and split the nori sheets in half.
6. If the sesame seeds are not already toasted, toast them in a pan over medium heat, stirring constantly until golden. Slice the avocado, cucumber, and imitation crab into strips.
7. Place half a sheet of nori on the rolling mat. With wet fingertips, spread a generous handful of sushi rice evenly over the entire surface.
8. Flip the nori so the rice faces down. Arrange the crab, avocado, and cucumber lengthwise across the center of the nori. Do not overfill.
9. Roll away from you, using the mat to shape and gently compress the roll. Sprinkle the outside with toasted sesame seeds, turning the roll on the mat to coat it.
10. Wipe a sharp knife with a damp paper towel. Cut the roll in half, align the halves, and slice into even pieces approximately 1 inch thick. Repeat with the remaining ingredients.
11. For optional spicy mayo, mix the mayonnaise and Sriracha, adjusting to taste. Serve the rolls with soy sauce, wasabi, and spicy mayo as desired.

### Notes:

- Equipment: A sushi rolling mat and plastic wrap are recommended. The original recipe suggests parchment paper as an alternative to a rolling mat.
- Use either prepared sushi vinegar or the homemade vinegar mixture—not both.
- Do not add salt to the rice cooking water; the seasoning comes from the sushi vinegar.
- Keep your fingertips wet when spreading the rice to prevent sticking.
- Avoid overfilling the rolls so they close properly.
- Form the rolls firmly enough to hold together when sliced.
- Wipe the knife with a damp paper towel before slicing to reduce sticking.
- The provided recipe does not specify the number of rolls or the quantities of nori and sesame seeds.

### Substitutions:

- Leave out the imitation crab if vegan and use vegan mayo.
