# Crepes

## Description

A light, thin French-style pancake made from a simple batter of flour, eggs, and milk. Crepes are versatile and can be filled with sweet or savory ingredients for breakfast, dessert, or a quick meal.

### Ingredients:

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- Butter or oil, for cooking

### Optional sweet filling:

- Nutella, peanut butter, jam, honey, or maple syrup
- Sliced bananas, strawberries, or berries
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

### Optional savory filling:

- Cheese, ham, spinach, mushrooms, or eggs
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### Steps for preparation:

1. Add flour, eggs, milk, water, salt, and melted butter to a blender. Blend until smooth.
2. Let the batter rest for 15–30 minutes for softer, more tender crepes.
3. Heat a non-stick pan or crepe pan over medium heat. Lightly butter the pan.
4. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter into the center of the pan.
5. Immediately tilt and rotate the pan so the batter spreads into a thin, even circle.
6. Cook for 1–2 minutes, until the edges lift and the bottom is lightly golden.
7. Flip the crepe carefully and cook for another 30–60 seconds.
8. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining batter, adding butter as needed.
9. Fill each crepe with your preferred sweet or savory filling.
10. Fold into quarters or roll it up, then serve warm.

### Notes:

- The first crepe may not turn out perfectly; use it to adjust the heat and batter amount.
- If the batter is too thick, add a little more milk or water.
- For a richer flavor, replace some of the milk with cream.
- Crepes can be made ahead and stored in the refrigerator for 2–3 days.

### Substitutions:

- Replace milk with non-dairy milk for a dairy-free version.
